For this section, you need to have a computer that meets the minimum requirements to run Adobe Lightroom. You can see the requirements at https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/system-requirements.html

Essentially:

**Windows:**

- Intel or AMD processor, 64-bit, 2 GHz or Faster
- Runs Microsoft Windows 10
- 2 GB of hard drive space

**Mac:**

- Multicore Intel 64-bit processor
- MacOS 10.13 or higher
- 2 GB of hard drive space

The version of Lightroom we need to use will not work on iOS, Android, ChromeOS, Linux, or other operating systems. You will need a laptop or desktop running Windows 10 or MacOS 10.13 or later.
